1. Who We Are

thegreatindiandeals.in is our main website and TheGreatIndiandeal is the deal-sharing name used across our content and social channels. The purpose of the platform is simple: help visitors discover online deals and offers without having to search across many websites.

2. Our Background

TheGreatIndiandeal originally operated as a Blogger-based deals and offers blog. thegreatindiandeals.in was launched in 2017 and continues the same broad goal of sharing useful online offers, now with a stronger focus on deal discovery and curated product information.

3. What We Do

  • Find and publish information about online deals, discounts, coupons, price drops and promotions.
  • Share selected product and offer links with visitors.
  • Publish deal alerts through Telegram, WhatsApp and Facebook.
  • Organize deal information so visitors can quickly open the relevant merchant page and check the live offer.
